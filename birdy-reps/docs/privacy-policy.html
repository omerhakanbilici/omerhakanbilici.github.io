Privacy Policy — Birdy Reps

Last updated: 10 September 2026

Birdy Reps is a game you play by doing push-ups in front of your phone's front camera. This page explains exactly what it does with data, and what it does not.

The camera

The game uses the front camera to find where your head is, so that moving your head moves the bird. This happens entirely on your device, using Apple's Vision framework.

No video or image is ever recorded, saved, or transmitted. Each camera frame is analysed in memory and discarded immediately. Nothing is written to storage, nothing is uploaded, and no image ever leaves your phone. There is no way for us — or anyone else — to see what your camera sees.

The game cannot be played without camera access. If you decline it, the app tells you so and does nothing else.

Data stored on your device

The app saves your high score and your settings (control sensitivity, sound on or off) in the device's own storage. This data stays on your phone, is not readable by other apps, and is deleted when you delete the app. It is never sent anywhere.

Advertising

The app is free and is supported by advertising, provided by Google AdMob. An interstitial ad may be shown on the game-over screen — at most one every three games, and never twice within a minute.

To serve those ads, Google may collect and process a device advertising identifier and related technical information. What Google does with it is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

On iOS, the app asks for your permission before any tracking identifier can be used, through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, ads are still shown but are not personalised, and the game behaves in exactly the same way. You can change this decision at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the app shows Google's consent form before requesting any ad, and your choice there governs what Google may do.

What the app does not do

  • It has no accounts and asks for no personal information — no name, no email, no sign-in.
  • It collects no analytics of its own.
  • It does not access your location, contacts, photo library, health data, or microphone.
  • It makes no network requests of its own. The only network traffic is Google's, for advertising.

Children

The app is rated 4+ and contains no objectionable content, but it is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them. Because the app collects no personal information at all, there is nothing for a parent to request the deletion of beyond deleting the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date at the top.
