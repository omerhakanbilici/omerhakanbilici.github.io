Birdy Reps — Support

A game you play by doing push-ups in front of your phone.

How it is played

Prop your phone up in front of you, facing you, and get into a push-up position. The front camera finds your head: push up and the bird climbs, come down and it drops. There is nothing to tap.

The bird will not move

Your head has to be in the camera's view. Move the phone back, or tilt it so that it can see you at the top of a rep as well as the bottom. If the game says Get back in frame, that is what it is telling you.

It says "Too dark"

The camera cannot find a face in a dark room, and there is no way around that — no front camera has a flash. Turn on a light. A lamp is enough; you do not need daylight.

The bird moves too much, or not enough

Settings → Sensitivity. It sets how far the bird travels for a given head movement. Raise it if you cannot reach the top and bottom of the screen; lower it if the bird shoots past the gap. The change takes effect on your next run.

Turning the sound off

Settings → Sound. The game plays over your own music rather than interrupting it, so you can leave a workout playlist running.

The camera

Nothing the camera sees is recorded, saved, or sent anywhere. Frames are analysed on your device and discarded immediately. The full detail is in the privacy policy.

Ads

The game is free and shows a full-screen ad now and then — never more than one every three games, and never twice inside a minute. You can change what advertisers are allowed to use in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on your phone; the game plays exactly the same either way.
